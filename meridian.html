THE JOB

  • Each day the Celestial Court pulls one new heist somewhere on Earth.
  • The thief flees through 4–7 transit cities, then holes up at the final stop.
  • You have a fixed number of HOURS. Every action burns time — flights can be delayed by weather, paperwork, fate.

THE LOOP

  • INTERVIEW witnesses: some describe the thief (hair, eyes, build, hobby, vehicle, a distinguishing feature), some hint at the NEXT city. Replies appear right in the panel — you can stop early and keep the hours.
  • FLIGHTS: fly anywhere. Every city looks innocent until you spend time canvassing it.
  • CASE BOARD: all 14 Court dossiers. Filter by heard traits; suspects contradicted by evidence are greyed out (ELIMINATED). When one remains it glows green — SOLE MATCH. Name them in the warrant right there. Re-issue freely — but only one arrest.
  • ARREST at the END of the trail with the RIGHT warrant. Wrong suspect or wrong city and the Court wins.

KEYS

  • 1–5 game actions
  • Esc close panel   Enter continue
  • M music   S sound fx (title screen)
  • Click any animation to skip it

SCORING

  • 500 + (10 × hours left) − (25 × wrong flights)
  • Daily cases build your STREAK. Cold Cases are practice — they don't count.
  • The clock runs out? The thief vanishes. Same outcome, no score.

HOUSE RULES

  • One case per day, same case for every detective on Earth (seeded by the date).
  • An original work: no affiliation with any famous globe-trotting franchise.
